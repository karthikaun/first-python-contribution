# Quiz 01

**1. You starred a repo. What changed in its code?**

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Nothing. A star is a bookmark; it doesn't touch the code.
</details>

**2. Why do you commit to your fork rather than to the original repo?**

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

You don't have permission to change the original. Your fork is your own
copy, and a pull request asks the original to take your commit.
</details>

**3. What's wrong with this line?** `name = "Ada Lovelace`

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

It's missing the closing quote, so it isn't valid Python.
</details>

**4. The bot commented that your PR changes files outside `first-commits/`.
What happened, and how do you fix it?**

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

You also changed another file. Undo that change in your fork and commit;
the PR updates by itself and the check runs again.
</details>
