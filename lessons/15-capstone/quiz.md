# Quiz 15

**1. Your PR to a project is open and approved but not merged yet. Can you
graduate?**

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Not yet. It has to be merged. Come back when it is.
</details>

**2. You fixed a bug in your own project and merged it. Does it count?**

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

No. The capstone needs a project you don't own, merged by a maintainer.
</details>

**3. Does a merged docs fix count?**

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Yes, as long as it's a real improvement the maintainers wanted.
</details>

**4. What's the test for whether a change is worth sending?**

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Would the maintainer have wanted it even if you weren't doing a course?
</details>

**5. Your contribution was on GitLab. How do you graduate?**

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Open an issue in the course repo with a link; the bot can only check GitHub,
so it's added by hand.
</details>
