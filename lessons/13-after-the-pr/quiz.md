# Quiz 13

**1. Your PR has had no response for four days. What do you do?**

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Nothing yet: a few days is normal. Make sure CI is green and comments are
answered. Nudge once after about two weeks (or what CONTRIBUTING says).
</details>

**2. A maintainer closes your PR: "We'd rather not add this." What's a
good reply?**

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

A short thank-you. If it's unclear, ask once whether a different, smaller
change would be welcome, then accept the answer.
</details>

**3. What does "Allow edits by maintainers" let maintainers do?**

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Push commits to your PR's branch, for example to finish small fixes
themselves.
</details>

**4. What does GitLab call a pull request?**

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

A merge request (MR).
</details>

**5. On Gerrit, a reviewer asks for a change. New commit or amend?**

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Amend your commit (keeping its `Change-Id:` trailer) and push it again with
`git push origin HEAD:refs/for/main`.
</details>
