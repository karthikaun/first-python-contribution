# Quiz 01

**1. Which of these is a real open source contribution?**
a) Fixing a typo in a project's docs
b) Reporting a bug with exact steps to reproduce it
c) Adding a missing test
d) All of the above

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

d) All of them. None needs clever code, and all are welcome.
</details>

**2. What is a pull request?**

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

A request asking a project's maintainers to pull your change into their
project. They review it and then merge it, ask for changes, or decline it.
</details>

**3. Why are small changes a good idea for your first contributions?**

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

They are quick to review, so they are more likely to be merged. A small
change still takes you through the whole workflow.
</details>

**4. Name two tools that most Python projects share.**

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Any two of: a virtual environment (venv), pytest, ruff (or another linter),
pre-commit.
</details>

**5. An AI agent can open a pull request for you. Give two reasons this course has you do every step by hand anyway.**

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Any two of: your name is on it, so you must be able to explain every line;
you need to read branches, commits and diffs to check what the agent did;
when a push is rejected or there's a conflict, you have to understand it to
fix it; and maintainers want to talk to you, in your own words.
</details>
